How savings are calculated

Cost: Royal Crystals converted to gold at your exchange rate.

Reward value: total gold value of the package contents.

Savings: reward value minus cost, expressed as gold and a percentage of reward value.

Medium shard pouches contain 2,000 shards and use ⅔ of the Large pouch price (3,000 shards). Bound gold is counted at face value unless adjusted.
